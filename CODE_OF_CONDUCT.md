# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CausalPilot AI adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone participating in the repository, issue tracker, reviews, discussions,
or project-related public spaces is expected to follow that standard.

In practical terms, be respectful, assume good faith while examining evidence,
give constructive feedback, accept responsibility for mistakes, and protect
other people's privacy. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized
attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private
information without permission are not acceptable.

Technical or statistical disagreement is welcome. Critique the method, claim,
test, or evidence—not the person.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer, **LAI ZEYU (来泽宇)**, may edit or remove contributions
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct violates
this policy. Responses will be proportionate to the context and impact.

Report conduct concerns through a private GitHub channel to the maintainer. If
the repository does not yet offer one, open a minimal public issue requesting a
private conduct-reporting channel and include no names, incident details, or
other sensitive information. Platform-level abuse may also be reported through
GitHub's own abuse-reporting tools.

This policy incorporates Contributor Covenant 2.1 by reference. Its Community
Impact Guidelines were inspired by Mozilla's code-of-conduct enforcement
ladder. Answers and translations are available from the [Contributor Covenant
FAQ](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/faq/).
