## Description

CausalPilot AI is an offline-first desktop workbench for declared randomized two-group business experiments. It helps analysts move from a local CSV to a locked analysis plan, a deterministic estimate, visible quality diagnostics, and an evidence-linked report.

Import a UTF-8 CSV, map the analysis unit, assigned treatment, and primary outcome, and confirm the randomized design. CausalPilot then calculates either an absolute risk difference for a binary outcome or a mean difference with Welch uncertainty for a continuous outcome. Results keep the effect estimate, confidence interval, sample size, diagnostic status, and practical business threshold together.

Use the built-in synthetic checkout-incentive experiment to explore the workflow without providing your own data. For local files, the desktop application invokes its bundled statistical engine directly. It does not start an analysis server or upload raw rows to the product author. You choose whether and where to save aggregate JSON or HTML evidence files.

Quality checks include required-field validation, duplicate analysis units, assignment encoding, missing or invalid outcomes, empty groups, sample-ratio mismatch, differential missingness, and sparse binary information. A diagnostic cannot prove that an experiment was operationally randomized, so the app requires the user to confirm the design and restricts its conclusion language when critical checks fail.

Marketing experiments are the primary use case. HR use is restricted to aggregate programme, policy, cohort, or team-level interventions. CausalPilot rejects requests framed as individual hiring, firing, promotion, compensation, performance, or employee-risk decisions.

Version 0.1.0 has no account, cloud sync, advertising, telemetry integration, subscription, or in-app purchase. Despite the product name, it does not use a generative-AI model at runtime; numeric results and narratives are produced by a deterministic local engine.

Important scope: CausalPilot 0.1.0 is designed for declared randomized two-arm experiments. It does not establish causality for observational data, provide legal or employment advice, or implement Difference-in-Differences, propensity-score matching, causal forests, or general regression modelling. Its reports support review and documentation; they do not guarantee a correct business decision or real-world impact.

CausalPilot AI was conceived and led by LAI ZEYU (来泽宇), who is the product author, evidence owner, and accountable QA owner. Development was materially assisted by AI coding tools; the released application itself does not call a generative-AI service.
